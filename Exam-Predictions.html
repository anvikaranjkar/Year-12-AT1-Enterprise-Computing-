The clearest clue: the school practice sequence

The Dispatch Performance activity asks you to apply two filters together, then clear them, calculate North and South averages with AVERAGEIF, and compare a chart. The IT Support Ticket activity asks you to retrieve a resolution time with exact VLOOKUP, apply an IF rule, and test a changed target. These activities closely match “filtering, summarising, looking up values and applying rules” in the teacher's advice.

That makes this a strong rehearsal sequence: filter → summarise a group → retrieve a record → classify against a rule → change an input → interpret the result. Treat it as a skill pattern that transfers to any enterprise, not a prediction that the actual test will use tickets or delivery zones.

Mini worked example for the new practice CSV

In the CSV, A is TicketID, B Region, C Category, D Urgency, E ResolutionMinutes, F TargetMinutes and G Cost. Headers occupy row 1 and twelve tickets occupy rows 2–13. Every row is one ticket. The data is fictional and the answer key below is for this practice only.

=AVERAGEIF(B2:B13,"North",E2:E13)
 =VLOOKUP("T208",A2:G13,5,FALSE)
 =IF(E2<=F2,"Met","Over target")
 =COUNTIF(E2:E13,">30")
 =SUM(G2:G13)

North mean: 28.20 minutes. South mean: 37.75 minutes. South is 9.55 minutes longer on average. T208 resolves in 60 minutes. Five tickets take more than 30 minutes. Total recorded cost is $748. North AND Network returns T201, T206, T209.

For a common target, put 25 in J2 and use =IF(E2<=$J$2,"Met","Over target"). Copy down, inspect the last formula and change J2 to 24. A 25-minute ticket changes from Met to Over target. A per-ticket target in F is a different rule; read which rule the question specifies.

What the five assessment criteria mean in your answers

  1. Systems meet enterprise needs: identify the enterprise problem and explain how the proposed feature addresses it.
  2. Data and information: show how raw ticket records become useful means, rates, flags and evidence.
  3. Tools and complex datasets: choose and apply correct filters, formulas, references and summaries.
  4. Innovative technologies: explain how a proposed technology changes a workflow, with a contextual benefit and limitation.
  5. Communicate to an audience: use a specific recommendation, clear units, understandable language and relevant evidence for that manager or user.

Write to the command word

Identify: name the requested item precisely. Compare: give both values, a meaningful difference and units. Explain: show how or why. Recommend/justify: give an action and connect evidence to its benefit. Evaluate/assess: weigh relevant benefits and limitations, then make a qualified judgement.

A useful recommendation pattern is action → numerical evidence → reason → enterprise benefit. For a limitation use issue → effect on evidence → consequence for the decision → improvement. For a system change use changed workflow → benefit → risk → response. Match the length to the marks; these are thinking structures, not guaranteed official marking allocations.

Section A · 24 marks

A1 · 3 marks

Filter Region to North AND Category to Network. State the matching ticket IDs and count. Explain the combined condition. Clear filters afterwards.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

T201, T206, T209:3 tickets. Both Region=North and Category=Network must be true for each included record.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for all matching IDs,1 for count,1 for correct AND explanation. Do not include a South Network ticket or a North Software ticket.

A2 · 4 marks

Use all twelve records. Write AVERAGEIF formulas for mean North and mean South resolution time. Give each result to two decimal places.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

=AVERAGEIF(B2:B13,"North",E2:E13) →28.20 minutes. =AVERAGEIF(B2:B13,"South",E2:E13) →37.75 minutes.

Suggested practice marking: 1 per aligned formula and 1 per result with unit. North:141/5=28.2. South:151/4=37.75. Exclude header row; a typed label needs straight quotation marks.

A3 · 2 marks

Calculate total recorded cost with a formula. State the result with a currency unit.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

=SUM(G2:G13) →$748.00.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for function and range;1 for the correctly labelled result. Cost is not revenue or profit.

A4 · 3 marks

Use exact VLOOKUP to retrieve ResolutionMinutes for T208. Give the formula, result and purpose of FALSE.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

=VLOOKUP("T208",A2:G13,5,FALSE) →60 minutes. FALSE requests an exact ID match.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for formula,1 for result/unit,1 for exact-match explanation. ResolutionMinutes is the fifth column of A:G. The lookup ID must be in the selected range’s first column.

A5 · 4 marks

In H2, use IF to show Met when ResolutionMinutes is no more than that ticket’s TargetMinutes, otherwise Over target. Fill down through H13. Give T201 and T206 labels and explain the equality boundary.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

=IF(E2<=F2,"Met","Over target"), filled down. T201:Met (18<=20). T206:Met (20<=20). Exactly equal meets the stated rule.

Suggested practice marking: 2 for correct rule, syntax and row references;1 for both requested labels;1 for the equality explanation. Each row uses its own F target; do not replace this with a global target.

A6 · 3 marks

Now use a different rule: J2 holds a common target of 25 minutes. In I2 write IF using the fixed J2 target, safe to fill down. For T203, state the output at 25, then after J2 changes to 24. Restore J2 to 25.

Section B · 16 marks

B1 · 4 marks

Using the four-week dashboard, compare North and South over-target rates. Explain why the raw over-target counts alone could mislead.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

North 60/200×100=30%; South 64/320×100=20%. North is 10 percentage points higher even though South has 4 more over-target tickets. South handles more tickets.

Suggested practice marking: 1 per rate,1 for correct percentage-point comparison,1 for explaining the different denominators. A 10-percentage-point gap is a 50% relative increase from 20% to 30%; do not interchange those statements.

B2 · 4 marks

Recommend where to trial a service improvement. Use at least two relevant dashboard figures and explain a benefit for the enterprise.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

Trial improved triage or dispatch support in North. Its over-target rate is 30% versus South’s 20%, and its mean resolution time is 32 minutes versus 27.5. A monitored trial could reduce delays and improve service reliability. Investigate case mix and costs before committing permanently; the data does not prove staffing is the cause.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for a specific feasible action/location,1 for relevant numerical evidence,1 for connecting the evidence to the action,1 for an enterprise benefit. Other well-supported recommendations can be valid.

B3 · 4 marks

Explain one specific data limitation, how it could affect your recommendation and what additional evidence you would collect.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

The dashboard omits case category and urgency. North may handle more complex tickets, so its longer times may not show poorer staff performance and simply adding staff may not solve the issue. Compare target rates and times within comparable categories/urgencies and collect workload, staffing and cost data before selecting the trial.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for a specific missing variable/limitation,1 for effect on interpretation,1 for consequence for the recommendation,1 for targeted additional evidence. An alternative is optional-survey bias: 25 North responses may not represent 200 tickets; explain non-response rather than asserting a known direction.

B4 · 4 marks

The enterprise proposes automated ticket routing with a live staff dashboard. Explain a changed workflow, a benefit, a risk and a suitable response.

Reveal the practice answer and self-marking guide

New tickets could be categorised and routed to staff with relevant skills, while a live dashboard highlights ageing queues. This may reduce manual sorting and improve response speed. Incorrect categories or a stale feed could misroute work and hide delays. Train staff, keep human override and a fallback process, show refresh status, and monitor over-target rate and routing errors during a trial.

Suggested practice marking: 1 for the process mechanism,1 for linked operational benefit,1 for a contextual risk,1 for a response or evaluation measure. Automation is a proposal here; the exact technology in the actual assessment is unknown.
